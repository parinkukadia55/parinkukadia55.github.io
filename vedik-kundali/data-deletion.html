Option 1: Immediate In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can instantly wipe your account and all stored horoscope charts directly on your phone:

  1. Open the Apni Astro application on your Android device.
  2. Tap your Profile / Account icon located in the top navigation bar.
  3. In the Account Dashboard, scroll down and tap the red "🗑️ Delete Account & Wipe Data" button.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account, saved birth charts, custom settings, and local database entries will be erased instantly from the device.
