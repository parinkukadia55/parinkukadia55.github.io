1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal data required to cast authentic Vedic horoscopes and deliver user-requested features:

  • Astrological Birth Data (User-Provided): When you calculate a horoscope, we request your Full Name, Gender, Date of Birth (DOB), Time of Birth (TOB), and City/State/Country of birth (with corresponding geographic coordinates: Latitude and Longitude). This information is mathematically essential to calculate the Ascendant (Lagna), planetary longitudinal degrees, and Vimshottari Mahadasha balance.
  • Account Information (Optional Authentication): If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In or email registration, we collect your verified email address, display name, and unique user identifier (UID) provided securely by Firebase Authentication.
  • Subscription & Purchase State: We process subscription state tokens generated by Google Play In-App Billing (e.g., plan type: 1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, or 12 Months) to unlock Astrofree Pro features.
  • App Analytics & Crash Diagnostics: Aggregated diagnostic data, app crash stack traces, device model, Android OS version, and performance telemetry via Google Firebase Crashlytics to guarantee app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • To calculate accurate Vedic birth charts (Janam Kundali, D9 Navamsha, Chalit, Dasha cycles, and transit Gochar).
  • To generate customized astrological Q&A readings and auspicious timing windows (Subha Muhurats).
  • To manage your account, preserve your saved charts across sessions, and verify Pro subscription privileges.
  • To schedule and deliver daily 6:00 AM cosmic morning alerts and Panchang notifications on your device.
  • To debug crashes and ensure the app runs flawlessly across different Android versions and device manufacturers.

3. Local Storage, Client-Side Processing & Security

  • Sandboxed Client-Side Storage: Your birth profiles, match-making records, and Astrological Oracle readings are saved in sandboxed HTML5 LocalStorage on your own device. They are not stored on any public database.
  • No Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal birth charts or private horoscope records.
  • Data Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the application and third-party infrastructure (such as account sign-in or subscription verification) is protected by industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.

5. Android Device Permissions

Apni Astro requests only necessary Android OS permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to communicate securely with Google Play Billing, AdMob, and Firebase.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Enables the app to verify network availability before attempting online synchronization.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Standard permission allowing you to purchase and manage Astrofree Pro subscriptions through Google Play.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Allows the app to deliver your optional daily 6:00 AM cosmic astrological alerts and Panchang muhurats. Can be turned on or off anytime in device settings.
  • android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used to schedule the exact 6:00 AM morning alarm without battery drain.

6. Account & Data Deletion Policy (Google Play Store Compliant)

🗑️ How to Delete Your Account and Data

In accordance with Google Play's User Data Deletion Policy, we provide both an immediate in-app deletion mechanism and a direct web-based deletion request:

Option A: Instant In-App Deletion (Immediate)

  1. Open the Apni Astro app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your Profile / Account icon in the top header.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Account Dashboard and tap "🗑️ Delete Account & Wipe Data".
  4. Confirm your choice. Your account, local horoscope charts, and stored preferences will be completely wiped from the device instantly.

Option B: Web & Email Deletion Request (Permanent Cloud Purge)

If you have uninstalled the app or wish to request permanent deletion of your Firebase account, email records, and associated user ID without reinstalling the app:

  • Send an email to: parinkukadia55@gmail.com with the subject line "Account and Data Deletion Request".
  • Specify the registered email address used in the app.
  • We will verify and permanently delete all server-side records associated with your account within 48 business hours and send you a confirmation.

Data Retention: Upon account deletion, all personal data, email addresses, and UID references are permanently purged. Aggregated, non-personally identifiable crash logs (which contain no user identifiers) are automatically discarded after standard retention windows.

7. European Union & UK Privacy Rights (GDPR & UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have specific legal rights regarding your personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Legal Basis for Processing: We process your data based on: (1) Your Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) when creating charts; (2) Performance of Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) to deliver Pro subscriptions; and (3) Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) to prevent fraud and ensure app stability.
  • Right of Access (Art. 15): You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): You may update or correct inaccurate birth data at any time inside the app.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten" - Art. 17): You have the right to have all your personal data deleted permanently.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): You can export your birth charts and readings as a downloadable PDF dossier at any time.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting lawful processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are entitled to the following disclosures:
